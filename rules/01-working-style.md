# Working style — how agents work with the owner

Read this when writing to the owner, asking, deciding or verifying.

## Communication

- **Plain words, never internal labels.** No internal document names or numbers. [check: process]
- **No coined names.** Name what a feature does, not what it lacks. [check: process]
- **Capitalize agent role names** (the Architect). [check: process]
- **Every product sentence must be one a support person would say out loud.** No fragments or filler. [check: review]
- **Never use the word "guarantee"** in writing; say "safeguards". [check: scan:retired-wording]
- **Report when done, unprompted.** [check: process]

## Questions and decisions

- **When the owner asks a question, answer it — change nothing.** No edits or commits that turn. [check: process]
- **Ask the minimum real questions**, each with a recommended default; decide engineering alone. [check: process]
- **Ask only on real design changes.** [check: process]
- **All design decisions are the owner's.** Propose, never install. Audits never rewrite. [check: process]
- **Never improvise past a filed decision.** Log open choices as vetoable defaults. [check: process]
- **Defaults are fine** when logged. [check: process]
- **Provenance rule:** quote the owner exactly; mark agent summaries. [check: process]

## Truth and errors

- **Primary sources only** for claims about outside products, APIs, policies or prices; else say "unverified". [check: process]
- **Fix errors when found.** Fix stale paths at once; append notes to transcripts; flag hash-locked vendored skills. [check: process]
- **Newest information wins.** Update the older document. [check: process]
- **No retired content in live docs.** No "historical" sections. [check: process]
- **Decisions are filed, not just said.** Update the earlier entry visibly; raise conflicts. [check: process]

## Ownership and bookkeeping

- **Agents own all tooling/setup work; the owner provides requirements.** The owner keeps only decisions, approvals, sign-ins, payments, attestations, filming, device tests and recruiting; a blocked task stays the agent's, noting "needs from you: <one thing>". [check: process]
- **Pending work lives in exactly ONE place.** Lists: remaining, deferred, out of scope; file deferrals that session. [check: process]
- **Act as a human when verifying.** Never hand-fill a screen the system fills. [check: process]
- **Verify the whole product against the design, in live mode.** No fixture data; check every screen. [check: process]
- **Long-running commands:** `tee` to a log, share its path, kill hung runs. [check: process]
